Prep Mastery

3 GOLDEN RULES OF FRESH MEAL PREP

How our scratch kitchen ensures every vegetable stays crispy, vibrant, and delicious for days.

💧

1. Separate Wet & Crisp

Always package acidic dressings and juicy diced tomatoes in separate leakproof cups until the moment you eat.

🌾

2. Heavy Grains At Base

Place whole grain carbs (quinoa, brown rice, roasted sweet potato) at the bottom so delicate leafy greens stay airy.

✨

3. Finish With Crunch

Add toasted pepitas, sourdough croutons, or walnut halves only right before eating to preserve maximum texture contrast.
