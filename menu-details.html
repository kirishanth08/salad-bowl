GREEN GODDESS SUPER BOWL

A nutrient-dense celebration of vibrant greens. Tender baby spinach and crisp cucumbers tossed with steamed edamame, sliced California Hass avocado, and our housemade avocado herb goddess dressing.

WHAT'S INSIDE THE BOWL

Every single component is prepared fresh without preservatives or refined oils.

Baby Spinach

Organic morning-harvested leaves, rich in folate, iron, and carotenoids.

Steamed Edamame

Young soybeans providing 12g of clean, complete plant protein.

Haas Avocado

Rich in heart-healthy monounsaturated oleic acid and potassium.

Toasted Pepitas

Roasted pumpkin seeds delivering zinc, magnesium, and crunch.

From the Head Chef

CHEF'S TASTING NOTES

"The secret to the Green Goddess bowl is the dual temperature interplay: chilled crisp cucumbers meet warm blanched edamame. We blend fresh Italian parsley, tarragon, chives, and cold-pressed olive oil right into ripe avocado to replace standard heavy mayonnaise."

Allergen Notice: Contains Soy (Edamame). Prepared in a clean facility that also handles tree nuts, dairy, and sesame.

Perfect Drink Pairing

Pairs harmoniously with our Green Immunity Juice (cucumber, ginger, celery) or a sparkling citrus kombucha.
