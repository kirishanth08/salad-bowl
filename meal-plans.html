Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about deliveries, freshness, and billing.

How long do the salad bowls stay fresh in my fridge? ▼

Because all dressings are packaged separately and greens are triple-washed and spun dry, our bowls remain crisp and vibrant for 5 full days from delivery day.

What if I am not home during the delivery window? ▼

No problem! Our insulated thermal tote bags and ice packs maintain refrigeration safe temperatures for up to 6 hours on your doorstep.

Can I customize every bowl in my weekly subscription? ▼

Yes! Balanced and Full Fresh plan members can either choose our chef signature bowls or build completely custom bowls in their subscriber portal.

When is the weekly cutoff to modify my bowl choices? ▼

You can modify your bowls, add extras, or pause delivery up until Monday at 11:59 PM before Wednesday's scheduled delivery.
