The Macro Equation

UNDERSTANDING THE BALANCED BOWL RATIO

We balance each bowl using the 40-30-20-10 nutritional blueprint:

40%

Crisp Leafy Greens & Veggies

High volume, micronutrient density, and prebiotic fiber for gut health.

30%

Complex Grains & Legumes

Slow-burning complex carbohydrates that stabilize blood sugar levels.

20%

Clean Lean Proteins

Free-range chicken, paneer, tofu, or chickpeas to fuel muscle recovery.

10%

Healthy Fats & Dressing

Cold-pressed olive oil and avocado assist fat-soluble vitamin absorption.

Safety Standards

ALLERGEN MANAGEMENT & KITCHEN SAFETY

We treat food sensitivities with utmost care. Our kitchen maintains strict sanitation protocols, separate color-coded cutting boards, and dedicated prep stations.

Please note that while we practice strict segregation, our facility handles sesame, tree nuts, and dairy. If you have an anaphylactic allergy, please alert our team via the contact form.
